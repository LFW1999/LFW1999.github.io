What constitutes as a "main" project?

A "main" project here is a project that was undertaken over a larger period of time, and demanded more work for its completion. By contrast, mini-projects are projects that were undertaken in a much shorter timeframe and were not as challenging to produce.
This page serves as a hub for all of my main projects undertaken so far that I am proudest of, and ideally showcase different engineering areas and experience to bolster any applications I have ongoing.
Unlike the Mini-Projects section, these projects will have a much deeper dive into the process and creation and also feature any technical reports produced during them, if applicable.
If these pages still dont provide enough information for you, please don't hesitate to reach out and ask! I'm always happy to discuss and debate them.

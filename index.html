"It's not a bug, It's an Undocumented Feature"

The purpose of this Portfolio is to provide myself and others a place to see all of my work in one area. This will include larger tasks undertaken whilst at university, as well as any personal projects I have taken an interest in.

Originally, I attended Aarhus University in Denmark, studying Economics & Business Management. The course was not quite what I was expecting, with a significant leap in both quantity and difficulty of mathematics involved - where I was more interested in Economic Theory. I studied there for a year before deciding it was best to pursue a different field. During my time in Denmark, I taught myself how to install electric motors on regular bicycles and flipped furniture to sell at a profit to fund my cost of living whilst abroad. It is because of this and my fascination with sci-fi robotics that I decided to study AI & Robotics at Staffordshire University, where I hoped to gain the skills necessary to have a hands-on approach in the field of Robotics.

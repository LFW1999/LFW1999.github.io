The purpose of this Portfolio is to provide myself and others a place to see all of my work in one area. This will include larger tasks undertaken whilst at university, as well as any personal projects I have taken an interest in.

Originally, I attended Aarhus University in Denmark, studying Economics & Business Management. Though I enjoyed the Economic Theory, my interest in engineering and robotics arose from my brief business venture into the electric bicycle market during my stay. I realised I had a passion for creating things with my hands, and so I decided to pursue a career in Robotics.
This path began with studying AI & Robotics at Staffordshire University, where I attained a 2:1 with Honours. Unfortunately, the course had practically no physical robotics in its content, leaving me very underwhelmed. As such I moved on to the University of Leeds studying Mechatronics & Robotics, wherein I was truly able to act on my passion and ideas for engineering through my projects. From this I attained a Pass with Merit, and now I am looking to further my knowledge and experience through a career in Industry or a PhD.
